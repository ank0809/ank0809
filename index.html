Hey You ❤️

Will you be my Valentine? 💕

💍

We met accidentally…
aur phir bas baatein start ho gayi 😊
bina plan ke, bina expectation ke.

Waqt guzarta gaya,
main tumhe jaanne laga…
aur pata hi nahi chala
kab tum meri aadat ban gaye 💫

Sach bataun to,
main ladkiyon se thoda door bhaagta tha 🙃
par tumse milne ke baad laga,
shayad sab log same nahi hote ❤️

Tum is generation se thodi alag ho ✨
tumhara behaviour,
tumhari thinking,
tumhari simplicity —
sab kuch bilkul mere type ka 🫶

Accidently mile hum,
par lagta hai destiny ne
mujhe jaan-boojh kar
tumse milwaya tha 😌❤️

Thank you for choosing me 🐻💖
