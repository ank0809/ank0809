Confession 💌

We met accidentally…
aur phir bas baatein start ho gayi 😊
bina plan ke, bina expectation ke.

Waqt guzarta gaya,
aur mujhe pata hi nahi chala
kab tum meri aadat ban gaye 💫

Tum bilkul meri soch jaise ho ✨
tumhara behaviour,
tumhari simplicity —
sab kuch bilkul mere type ka 🫶

Accidently mile hum,
par lagta hai destiny ne
mujhe jaan-boojh kar
tumse milwaya tha 😌❤️
